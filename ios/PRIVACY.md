# PC Control privacy policy

PC Control communicates directly with your paired Windows PC over your local network using TLS. The app does not collect or transmit information to the developer, operate a cloud relay, include analytics or advertising, or require an account.

Pairing credentials and the PC certificate fingerprint are stored in iOS Keychain on your device. Display and trackpad preferences are stored locally. Typing, mouse movement, and control commands are transmitted to your paired PC to perform the requested action. The app does not persist typed text or input history.

Local network access discovers and controls your computer. Camera access is optional and is used only to scan the pairing QR code. Camera images are not stored or uploaded. A pairing file can be imported instead. Forgetting a PC deletes its stored pairing credential from this app.

Apple may process device and diagnostic information as part of iOS, App Store, or TestFlight services under Apple's policies.
